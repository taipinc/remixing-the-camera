# Where to go from here?

## General Resources:

- [unthinking.photography](https://unthinking.photography/): Unthinking Photography is The Photographers' Gallery's online resource that explores photography's increasingly automated, networked life.

- [We Make Money Not Art](https://we-make-money-not-art.com/): a blog by curator Régine Debatty that covers the intersection of art, science, and technology.

- [Neural](https://neural.it/): an online magazine that explores the intersection of art, science, and technology, with a focus on digital culture and media art.

- [CreativeApplications.Net](https://www.creativeapplications.net/): a platform that showcases creative coding projects, tools, and resources for artists and designers.

- [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/): digital media company exploring the ways technology is shaping–and is shaped by–our world.

- [Antikythera Journal](https://journal.antikythera.org/): a peer-reviewed journal focused on the reorientation of planetary computation as a philosophical, technological, and geopolitical force.

## For learning more:

- [The Coding Train](https://thecodingtrain.com/): a YouTube channel by Daniel Shiffman that offers tutorials on creative coding, including topics like machine learning and generative art ([YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@TheCodingTrain)).

- [Artificial Images](https://artificial-images.com/): Demos and explanations to make art using machine learning ([YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ArtificialImages)).

## For more:

- [Are:na](https://www.are.na/): a platform for connecting and sharing ideas, where users can create and explore collections of content on various topics, including art, design, and technology.

- [Course Are:na Channel](https://www.are.na/shabtai-pinchevsky/photography-emerging-technologies): a channel created for this course, showcasing resources, projects, and ideas related to emerging technologies in photography and art.
